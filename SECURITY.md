# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not disclose suspected security vulnerabilities in public GitHub issues, discussions, screenshots, or social-media posts.

Use the official contact or support channel at [elephantsdata.com](https://elephantsdata.com) and include:

- A clear description of the suspected issue
- The affected feature or URL
- Reproduction steps using non-sensitive test data
- The potential impact
- Any supporting screenshots with credentials, tokens, email addresses, tenant identifiers, and customer data removed

Do not access, alter, download, or retain data that does not belong to you. Do not perform destructive testing or testing that could disrupt the service or another customer.

ElephantsData will review responsibly submitted reports and may request additional information. This document does not represent a bug-bounty program, certification, service-level agreement, or authorization to test third-party providers.
